## 3. SOLUTION

**Section label:** How Frontier Intelligence is different

**Headline:**
### Your personal intelligence analyst — connected to everything, reporting to you.

**Body:**
Frontier Intelligence isn't another aggregator. It's a structured intelligence pipeline built around your specific focus area. You define your workspace — your topics, your sources, your signal priorities. The system does the rest: monitoring, clustering, synthesizing, and surfacing what matters.

The result isn't a feed. It's a briefing. Organized by theme, ranked by significance, backed by traceable sources — delivered directly to your Claude environment so you can interrogate it, extend it, and act on it.

**Three value cards:**

**[Icon: Target]**
**Focused, not broad**
Create multiple workspaces for different domains — competitive landscape, regulatory changes, technology shifts. Each workspace learns your signal priorities over time.

**[Icon: Layers]**
**From raw signal to structured insight**
LLM-powered clustering groups related signals across sources. You see trend clusters, not individual articles — with source counts, timelines, and key actors.

**[Icon: Plug]**
**Native Claude integration via MCP**
Ask questions, run analysis, build reports — all from within Claude. Frontier Intelligence connects directly to your workflow through the Model Context Protocol.

## 4. HOW IT WORKS

**Section label:** Three steps to structured intelligence

**Headline:**
### From setup to your first briefing in under 30 minutes.

---

**Step 01 — Build Your Workspace**

Define what you're watching. Add your Telegram channels, RSS feeds, web sources, and email newsletters. Set your focus topics and signal keywords. Frontier Intelligence starts monitoring immediately — no engineering required.

*Visual: workspace setup screen with source connectors and topic fields*

**Step 02 — Intelligence Gets Structured**

Every few hours, the system processes new signals through an LLM pipeline. Related signals cluster into trend groups. Each cluster gets a significance score, a summary, a timeline, and a source list you can verify. You see patterns, not noise.

*Visual: cluster view showing "Agentic AI infrastructure" trend with 14 signals, 6 sources, rising since Tuesday*

**Step 03 — Ask Claude Anything**

Your Frontier workspace connects to Claude via MCP. Open Claude, ask "What's the most important shift in my focus area this week?" — and get a sourced, structured answer in seconds. Export it. Share it. Build on it.

## SUPPLEMENTARY ELEMENTS

### FAQ (collapsed accordion, bottom of page)

**Q: What sources can I connect?**
A: Telegram channels and groups, RSS feeds, individual web pages and blogs, email newsletters (via forwarding address), and public social feeds. Enterprise plans support private sources and custom ingestion.

**Q: How is this different from Google Alerts or Feedly?**
A: Alerts and RSS readers give you a list of items. Frontier Intelligence gives you a structured intelligence brief — signals clustered by theme, ranked by significance, synthesized into insight. The difference is between raw data and analysis.

**Q: How does the Claude MCP integration work?**
A: Once connected, your Frontier workspaces appear as context sources inside Claude. You can ask natural language questions and get answers grounded in your specific signal database — with citations. No copy-paste required.

**Q: How current are the signals?**
A: Strategist and Team plans monitor sources every 2 hours. Analyst plan updates daily. For time-critical monitoring, we recommend Strategist tier and above.

**Q: Can I use Frontier for a client project?**
A: Yes. Strategist plan supports 5 separate workspaces — each can be scoped to a specific client engagement, topic, or geographic market.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Your workspace, sources, and signals are private to your account. We do not use your data to train models or share it across accounts. Enterprise plans include custom data residency options.
